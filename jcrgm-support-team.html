How it works

Four steps, from need to receipt

  1. 1
    Apply, attach documents & be registeredA member applies on their phone with a photo of their NRC/ID and proof of the need, or the Support Team registers a beneficiary being sponsored.
  2. 2
    Vetting & referenceTwo team members verify the details — the documents, a home visit or a phone call to the referee.
  3. 3
    Decision & paymentThe committee approves, declines or defers. Money is paid as far as possible straight to the school or clinic.
  4. 4
    Receipt & follow-upA proof reference is recorded, a review date is set, and the sponsor is told what became of their gift.

Before you apply

What we ask of you — and what we promise

⚠️
Apply honestly. Every case is checked, including the documents you attach. A false claim or a forged document ends support immediately and is reported to church leadership. A genuine need, however small, is always welcome.
🔒
Your privacy. Names, phone numbers, NRC details and uploaded documents are seen only by the Support Team and are held under the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021. Your ID is used to verify who you are — it is never published, and never shared with a sponsor.
💰
No cash is ever promised. Support is given as far as possible by paying the school, clinic or supplier directly, and everything is receipted.
🚨
In an emergency — a child at risk, violence at home, or a medical crisis — do not wait for this form. Call the office now, or go to the nearest police station or hospital.

    Good practice

    Standing orders of this desk

    1
    Two-person rule. No disbursement is made on one signature. The assigned member and one other must both appear in the file notes.
    2
    Pay the institution, not the pocket. School fees go to the bursar, treatment to the clinic. Record the receipt number the same day.
    3
    Close the loop. Every applicant is told the outcome, and every partner is thanked on the channel they chose.
    4
    Children and the vulnerable. Where a minor is involved, a guardian's consent must be ticked and the ID attached must be the guardian's.
    5
    Documents are the most sensitive thing here. Open a document only when you need it for vetting. Never forward an ID image on WhatsApp, never keep it on a personal phone, and delete the case file's documents once the case is closed (button in the case drawer).

    Where the data lives. Cases, partner records and uploaded documents are saved in this church app's own storage on the device — under the same rules as the rest of the church app. In the demo build this is the browser's IndexedDB (falling back to localStorage, then memory), and it can be pointed at the church's Supabase project through CONFIG.apiBase at the top of the script.
    What the public form can do. It can file a case with documents, register one partner, and look up one case with the reference number plus the last four digits of the phone that applied. It cannot list or browse anyone else's file.
    Document retention. ID images and bills are kept only as long as the case is open plus the audit period, then deleted. Use Purge documents in the case drawer once the case is closed.
    Outbound messages. Approving a partner opens a ready-written thank-you on WhatsApp or in the email app. To send it automatically from the church's own WhatsApp Business number or mail domain, set CONFIG.notifyEndpoint — the desk will post the approved partner and message there and record the result.
