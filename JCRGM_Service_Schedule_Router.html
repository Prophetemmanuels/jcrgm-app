1. Church Leaders / Pastors

The moment a name is added it is placed into one of the four service groups automatically, so one person can never appear on another group's Sunday.

Shuffle Groups re-deals the same names into new groups. Nobody is removed.

2. Group Rotation

How the Sundays rotate
1st Sunday → Group 1  •  2nd Sunday → Group 2
3rd Sunday → Group 3  •  4th Sunday → Group 4
A 5th Sunday starts again at Group 1.

Each group leads both the Morning Service and the Afternoon Prophetic Ministry on its own Sunday, so no two groups ever clash on the same day.

A Friday Midweek Service is led by the group that is taking the Sunday of that same week.

Every time a group serves, the duties are re-optimised so that nobody keeps a responsibility they already carried earlier in the month. If a group has fewer leaders than the responsibilities on the list, some names must carry more than one duty — the router then spreads them as widely as possible and reports it under Repeat Guard.
