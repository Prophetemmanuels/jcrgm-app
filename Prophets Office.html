Inside the office

A place of order, reverence and honest counsel

The Prophet’s Office exists so that no one who genuinely needs a word, a prayer or sound counsel has to fight through a crowd to receive it. Under the leadership of Prophet Emmanuel Mulenga, the office runs a proper appointment diary, a trained intercessory team and a follow-up desk.

Every request that comes through WhatsApp is logged, prayed over and answered by a real person. You will always know your date, your time, who will attend to you and what to bring — before you leave your house.
