September 2026

A strategic night of prayer, redemption, spiritual renewal, prophetic ministry and personal encounter in the presence of God.

What is Redemption?

Redemption speaks of God's saving work through Jesus Christ, bringing people from bondage into freedom, restoration and new life.

The Power of Persistent Prayer

Persistent prayer expresses steadfast faith, dependence upon God and continued seeking of His will. As taught in Luke 18:1-8 , believers are encouraged not to lose heart in prayer.

The Night of the Redeemed is a continuing quarterly convocation. Its season numbers are permanent and sequential. The numbering does not reset when a new calendar year begins.
