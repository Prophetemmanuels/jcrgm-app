What is Redemption?

Redemption is the ultimate legal divine exchange. We, who were once bound to spiritual insolvency and captivity, have been purchased back and restored into complete alignment with God's original master design. It is not a passive rescue; it is a ransom paid in full by the blood of Jesus Christ, changing our status from slaves of sin to heirs of the King.

The Power of Persistent Prayer

Persistent prayer is the unyielding posture of absolute faith that refuses to settle for anything less than Heaven’s answer. As illustrated in Luke 18:1-8, consistency in prayer is not attempting to bend God's will; it is building a solid spiritual conduit of authority that dismantles persistent dark configurations and demands dynamic supernatural outcomes in our lives.
