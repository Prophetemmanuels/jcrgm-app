✂️ Tailoring Specifications — Ladies
  • ✔ Length: Below the knee minimum (modesty standard; 2 inches below kneecap).
  • ✔ Sleeves: Tailored three-quarter or full-length; no sleeveless or sheer arms.
  • ✔ Neckline: High boatneck, Peter Pan collar, or elegant mock-neck.
  • ✔ Accents: Pussy-bow silk scarf, fabric rosette brooches, or waist belts.
  • ✔ Footwear: Modest closed-toe court pumps or clean white sneakers on designated days.
👔 Tailoring Specifications — Gentlemen
  • ✔ Cut: Tailored modern fit; well-ironed twill dress shirts and pressed trousers.
  • ✔ Collars: Crisp spread collar or Mandarin band collar with gold piping.
  • ✔ Ties: Satin/silk necktie matching the designated Sunday palette or bow stole.
  • ✔ Accessories: Solid brass ministry crest pin; genuine leather dress belt.
  • ✔ Footwear: Polished black/brown leather oxfords or pristine spotless white sneakers.
🇿🇲 African Chitenge Wax Print Standards
  • ✔ Fabric: 100% Genuine cotton Zambian wax print or Dutch block print.
  • ✔ Ladies Gele: Sculpted headwrap or crown gele matching the dress motifs.
  • ✔ Men Safari: Bespoke Chitenge short/long sleeve shirt with dark solid trousers.
  • ✔ Lining: Fully lined interior bodice for sweat absorption and structural drape.
