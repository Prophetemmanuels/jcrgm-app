JCRGM Recommended Study Method

  1. Pray for wisdom and understanding.
  2. Read the assigned biblical passage several times.
  3. Observe what the text actually says.
  4. Interpret the passage within its literary and historical context.
  5. Compare Scripture with Scripture.
  6. Write your observations.
  7. Apply biblical truth responsibly.
  8. Discuss difficult questions with mature teachers and leaders.
