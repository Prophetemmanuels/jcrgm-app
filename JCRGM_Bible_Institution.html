Regulations

Student handbook

Read it before you submit your first assignment. Registration is agreement to it.

1. The covenant of study

Every admitted student makes a covenant before God and the church: a course or a level takes one year to complete. You will be serious and focused — present in the Word before you are present on the platform.

  • A minimum of 12 focused study hours per term is expected of each course.
  • Lessons are worked in order; the assignment is unlocked when the lessons are complete.
  • Late work is accepted with a written reason; unexplained silence for 90 days suspends enrolment.

2. Assignments & essays

Each course ends with 20 multiple-choice questions and one essay. When every question has an answer, press Check my answers: the objective mark is shown at once and the paper is sealed. From that moment your choices stand for the rest of the course and cannot be changed, and the essay is then marked by the registrar against the published rubric.

  • A sealed Part A is the student's final word on the objective paper; only the registrar can lift the seal by returning the paper for correction.
  • Minimum word counts and required elements are printed with every assignment.
  • Plagiarism, invented quotations or unverified footnotes return the assignment to zero and are reported to the overseers.
  • One resubmission is allowed within 30 days of feedback.

3. Examinations

The level examination is sat once. It is timed; the clock does not stop for a second sitting. The invigilator's code is given at the church office on the day.

  • A pass is 60% (65% at masters and prophetic level, 70% at doctoral level).
  • A failed paper may be retaken after 30 days, once.
  • Cheating forfeits the level and the fee.

4. Fees, certificates & records

Courses and lessons are free of charge to members of the ministry. The certificate fee is K150, paid to the church Airtel Money line, and it covers printing, the register entry and the registrar's verification.

  • Pay to 0979 554 970, account name Emmanuel Sakala, using your student ID as the reference.
  • Claim the payment in the Certificate tab; the seal is released when it is verified.
  • Transcripts are issued at no charge; duplicates of a sealed certificate cost K150.

5. Conduct & the ministry track

Prophetic and practical courses are ministered under supervision. Hours are logged, samples are submitted, and an overseer signs off each report.

  • No prophecy is uttered over a person for money, fear or favour.
  • Words given in the assembly are judged by the elders, and every spirit is subject to the prophets.
  • Unrepentant sin in the student's life pauses the level; it does not end the fellowship.

6. The recommended study method

  1. Pray for wisdom and understanding before you open the text.
  2. Read the assigned passage several times — aloud, and slowly.
  3. Observe what the text actually says, before you decide what it means.
  4. Interpret it within its literary and historical context.
  5. Compare Scripture with Scripture; clearer texts govern weaker ones.
  6. Write your observations out by hand or in the notes panel.
  7. Apply the truth responsibly, to your own life first.
  8. Discuss the difficult questions with mature teachers, not only online.

7. Records, and the day the school grows

Your record lives in this file, on this device: lessons read, the 20-question papers, your essays, the marks, the examination and the fee claim. The registrar’s console reads the same record, which is how marking and the seal are done.

When the school moves to a database and an online portal, this file remains the student’s copy: the transcript and the certificate may be printed or exported at any time, and the register may be exported as CSV for the church’s books.

8. Devices, privacy & data

This school is a single offline file: your work is kept on this device and in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no server to trust.

  • Clearing the browser's site data erases your record; print or export it before you change devices.
  • The registrar's console is protected by a staff code and must not be left open on a shared device.
  • Keep a paper or printed copy of your transcript and certificate with your church records.

9. Speaking with the Administration

Every enquiry goes through the Registrar page, where a form composes your message and opens it on WhatsApp to the office line +260 97 955 4970 (0979 554 970, account name Emmanuel Sakala). Tick Add my record so far and the office receives your enrolment, your marks and your fee position with it.

  • Attach a photograph of the Airtel Money confirmation in the WhatsApp thread; quote your student ID as the reference.
  • By e-mail the office is [email protected]; the same form will compose it for you.
  • Write plainly and once: what happened, what you need, and when. Answers are given in the same thread, and admission, marking and the seal are not decided by whoever asks loudest.
