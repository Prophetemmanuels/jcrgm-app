How to add books
  1. Create a folder named books in your GitHub repo.
  2. Upload PDF files into that folder (e.g. Pilgrims-Progress.pdf).
  3. Edit the CONFIG block in this file with your GitHub username & repo name.
  4. Commit & push. The library refreshes automatically.

Our Mission

A Refuge for the Reading Saint

✦

The JCRGM Library — a ministry of Jesus Christ Redeems Global Ministries — exists to make Christ-centered literature freely accessible to every member of our congregation and the wider body of Christ across the nations. From the writings of the Reformers to modern devotionals, we curate works that build faith, deepen understanding, and encourage holy living, that Christ may be known, loved, and proclaimed globally.

"A reader today, a leader tomorrow."
