Purpose of this document

This Constitution establishes the spiritual, governance, administrative, financial, membership, departmental, safeguarding and operational framework of Jesus Christ Redeems Global Ministries.

PREAMBLE

PREAMBLE AND FOUNDATIONAL DECLARATION

Declaration

We, the members of Jesus Christ Redeems Global Ministries, acknowledging God Almighty as Creator and Sovereign Lord, confessing Jesus Christ as Lord and Saviour, and recognizing the authority of Holy Scripture, hereby establish this Constitution to regulate the spiritual, administrative, financial and operational affairs of the Church.

We commit ourselves to the proclamation of the Gospel, making disciples, strengthening families, developing Christian character, prayer, worship, evangelism, missions, service to humanity and responsible stewardship.

“Upon this rock I will build my church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.” — Matthew 16:18
CHAPTER 1

IDENTITY, NAME AND PURPOSE

The name shall be JESUS CHRIST REDEEMS GLOBAL MINISTRIES (JCRGM). The Church may establish branches, ministries, departments, missions, institutions and projects under its authority.

  • Christian religious organization.
  • Non-profit ministry.
  • Church resources shall serve approved ministry purposes.
  • Members do not personally own Church assets by virtue of membership or office.
  • The Church shall operate according to its registration status and applicable law.

Vision: To raise a spiritually mature, Christ-centred and empowered people who fulfil God's purposes and positively influence families, communities, the nation and the world.

Mission: Preach the Gospel; make disciples; teach Scripture; develop prayerful believers; equip workers; strengthen families; reach children and youth; support vulnerable persons; plant churches; and participate in missions and community transformation.

Christ-centred faith, biblical truth, prayer, integrity, excellence, servanthood, accountability, love, unity, holiness, compassion, faith, order and stewardship.

CHAPTER 2

STATEMENT OF FAITH AND DOCTRINE

Articles 5–12

  1. Holy Scripture: The Bible is inspired Scripture and authoritative for faith and conduct.
  2. Triune God: One God eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
  3. Jesus Christ: Son of God, fully divine and fully human, born of the virgin Mary, crucified, risen, ascended and coming again.
  4. Salvation: By God's grace through faith in Jesus Christ, expressed in repentance and transformed living.
  5. Holy Spirit: The Spirit convicts, regenerates, sanctifies, empowers, guides and distributes spiritual gifts.
  6. Church: The body of Christ called to worship, evangelize, disciple and serve.
  7. Ordinances: Baptism and Holy Communion shall be observed according to JCRGM doctrine and ministerial practice.
  8. Christian Conduct: Members shall pursue holiness, honesty, love, humility, reconciliation and integrity.
CHAPTER 3

MEMBERSHIP

Membership Categories

  1. Regular Members
  2. Associate Members
  3. Children
  4. Youth
  5. Workers
  6. Ministers
  7. Honorary Members

Admission

A person may be received into membership through the Church's approved process, profession of Christian faith, completion of required orientation and agreement with this Constitution and doctrine.

Rights

  • Attend worship.
  • Receive pastoral care.
  • Participate in approved ministries.
  • Receive teaching and spiritual development.
  • Raise concerns through proper channels.
  • Be treated with dignity and fairness.

Responsibilities

  • Live according to Christian principles.
  • Support Church unity and mission.
  • Serve according to gifts and capacity.
  • Protect Church property.
  • Maintain appropriate confidentiality.
  • Support the Church through prayer, service and giving.
CHAPTER 4

LEADERSHIP AND GOVERNANCE

Governance Structure

  1. Overseer / Presiding Minister
  2. Senior Pastoral Leadership
  3. Presbytery / Council of Ministers
  4. Church Administrative Council
  5. Department Heads
  6. Branch Leadership
  7. Ministry Workers
  8. General Membership

Leadership Principles

  • Leadership is service.
  • Authority must be exercised responsibly.
  • Leaders remain accountable.
  • Leaders shall avoid conflicts of interest.
  • Church office shall not be used for unlawful personal enrichment.
  • Leaders shall maintain confidentiality and safeguarding standards.

Qualifications

Leaders should demonstrate Christian character, doctrinal soundness, spiritual maturity, faithfulness, integrity, competence, teamwork and willingness to submit to accountability.

CHAPTER 5

OFFICE OF THE OVERSEER

Role

The Overseer provides spiritual direction, doctrinal oversight, pastoral leadership and strategic direction for JCRGM.

Responsibilities

  • Guard the spiritual vision.
  • Preside over major spiritual and administrative meetings.
  • Appoint or recommend senior leaders according to Church procedure.
  • Provide pastoral and spiritual oversight.
  • Represent the Church where authorized.
  • Approve major ministry initiatives.
  • Promote unity and leadership development.

Accountability

Spiritual authority does not remove financial, legal, safeguarding or administrative accountability. The Overseer shall work with the appropriate governing bodies to ensure responsible governance.

CHAPTER 6

CHURCH DEPARTMENTS AND MINISTRIES

Every department shall have an approved leader, defined responsibilities, appropriate records and reporting requirements. No department is independent of JCRGM unless separately and lawfully constituted.
CHAPTER 7

DISCIPLINE & RESTORATION

Purpose

Discipline seeks truth, protection, repentance, restoration, justice and Church order, never revenge or humiliation.

Grounds

  • Serious misconduct.
  • Fraud or theft.
  • Abuse or exploitation.
  • Persistent disruptive conduct.
  • Serious doctrinal violation.
  • Misuse of Church funds.
  • Sexual misconduct.
  • Falsification of records.
  • Conduct seriously damaging to the Church.

Process

  1. Receive and document concern.
  2. Assess immediate safeguarding needs.
  3. Conduct fair inquiry.
  4. Give the person opportunity to respond.
  5. Determine action.
  6. Document outcome.
  7. Provide appeal where applicable.
  8. Pursue restoration where possible.
Criminal, abuse or immediate-danger allegations must not be concealed as internal Church discipline. Applicable legal and reporting obligations must be followed.
CHAPTER 8

FINANCE, TITHES, OFFERINGS & ACCOUNTABILITY

Sources

  • Tithes.
  • Offerings.
  • Freewill contributions.
  • Donations.
  • Approved fundraising.
  • Grants where appropriate.
  • Approved Church activities.
  • Other lawful sources.

Financial Controls

AreaRule

RecordingCollections should be recorded promptly.
BankingFunds should be deposited without unnecessary delay.
PaymentsPayments require appropriate authorization and documentation.
ReconciliationAccounts should be reconciled regularly.
ReportingPeriodic reports shall be presented to the appropriate governing body.

Tithes and Offerings

The Church shall teach biblical stewardship and encourage giving freely and responsibly. No person shall be coerced through threats, humiliation or manipulation.

CHAPTER 9

PROPERTY, ASSETS & PROJECTS

Ownership

Property acquired for JCRGM shall be held and administered for Church purposes and not private benefit.

Asset Register

Maintain records of land, buildings, vehicles, equipment, furniture, instruments, computers and other material assets.

Disposal

Major assets may only be sold, transferred, donated or disposed of through the required Church approvals and applicable law.

CHAPTER 10

MEETINGS & DECISION MAKING

Meetings

  • Leadership meetings.
  • Pastoral meetings.
  • Departmental meetings.
  • Workers' meetings.
  • General Church meetings.
  • Special meetings.

Minutes

Material decisions shall be recorded with attendees, agenda, resolutions and action points.

Quorum

Unless this Constitution states otherwise, a committee's quorum shall be determined by its approved terms of reference.

CHAPTER 11

APPOINTMENTS, ELECTIONS & SUCCESSION

Ministry positions may be filled by appointment, election, ordination or commissioning according to the nature of the office.

Selection Criteria

  • Character.
  • Spiritual maturity.
  • Competence.
  • Faithfulness.
  • Agreement with doctrine.
  • Ability to serve under authority.

Succession

The Church shall maintain leadership development and succession planning to reduce disruption when leaders resign, retire, become incapacitated or leave office.

CHAPTER 12

RECORDS & INFORMATION

Records

  • Membership register.
  • Baptism records.
  • Marriage records where maintained.
  • Leadership register.
  • Minutes.
  • Financial records.
  • Asset register.
  • Property documents.
  • Official correspondence.
  • Department reports.
  • Incident and safeguarding records.

Confidentiality

Sensitive pastoral, disciplinary, safeguarding and personal information shall only be accessed by authorized persons.

CHAPTER 13

DISPUTE RESOLUTION

  1. Private discussion where appropriate.
  2. Mediation by a suitable leader.
  3. Referral to Pastoral Council.
  4. Formal review where necessary.
  5. External professional or legal process where appropriate.

No member shall be punished merely for raising a genuine concern in good faith.

CHAPTER 14

SAFEGUARDING, CHILD PROTECTION & SAFETY

Zero Tolerance

JCRGM shall maintain a zero-tolerance position toward abuse, exploitation, sexual misconduct, harassment, trafficking and deliberate harm against children or vulnerable persons.

Responsibilities

  • Screen appropriate workers.
  • Provide safeguarding training.
  • Maintain safe supervision.
  • Establish reporting channels.
  • Protect complainants from retaliation.
  • Keep appropriate records.
  • Cooperate with lawful investigations.

Children

Children's ministry shall operate with age-appropriate supervision, safe environments, responsible adult leadership and appropriate parent/guardian communication.

BRANCHES, ASSEMBLIES & CHURCH PLANTING

Establishment

Leadership

Reporting

  • Finance.
  • Department activity.
  • Evangelism.
  • Major incidents.
  • Annual ministry report.

LEGAL & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

Responsibilities

  • Monitor statutory returns.
  • Maintain current office-bearer information.
  • Record constitutional amendments.

CHAPTER 15

AMENDMENT OF THE CONSTITUTION

Proposed amendments shall be submitted in writing, reviewed for theological, administrative, financial and legal implications, and approved through the Church's prescribed governance process. Where required, statutory notification or approval shall be completed before an amendment becomes effective.

The identity of Jesus Christ as Head of the Church, the biblical foundation and the core mission shall not be altered in a manner contrary to JCRGM's foundational faith.

CHAPTER 16

DISSOLUTION

The Church may only be dissolved through the lawful process applicable to its registration status and after approval by the highest authorized Church body.

After lawful liabilities are settled, remaining assets shall be transferred to a lawful Christian or charitable organization with substantially consistent objectives, subject to applicable law. Assets shall not be distributed among members merely because of membership.
