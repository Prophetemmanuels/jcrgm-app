Quick Start

10-step daily rhythm

1. Prepare — remove distractions and pray for wisdom.
2. Read — read the passage slowly at least twice.
3. Observe — notice repeated words, commands, people, contrasts and cause/effect.
4. Context — examine the paragraph, chapter, book, audience and setting.
5. Interpret — determine what the text meant before applying it personally.
6. Compare — cross-reference Scripture and compare translations.
7. Meditate — slowly reflect on what is actually written.
8. Apply — identify one belief, action, correction or practice.
9. Pray — turn the truth into worship, confession and intercession.
10. Remember & teach — memorize the key truth and explain it in your own words.

Study Anchor

Interpretation guardrails

Meaning before message.

  • Read the whole paragraph/chapter.
  • Identify speaker, audience and purpose.
  • Respect literary genre.
  • Compare translations.
  • Use cross-references carefully.
  • Separate clear teaching from inference.
  • Do not make an impression override the written text.

7-stage memory method

  1. Read aloud 5 times.
  2. Understand the context.
  3. Break the verse into phrases.
  4. Recall with the text hidden.
  5. Write it from memory.
  6. Use it in prayer and conversation.
  7. Review on 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days.

Do not memorize only sounds. Memorize meaning, context and truth.

Discernment filter

Does it agree with Scripture?
Is the context understood?
Is it consistent with Christlike character?
Have I distinguished fact from impression?
Have I prayed and waited?
Have I sought mature counsel when appropriate?

Meditation is not inventing a message. It is slowing down enough to understand and obey God's Word.
