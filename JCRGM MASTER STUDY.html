Quick Start

10-step daily rhythm

1. Prepare — remove distractions and pray for wisdom.
2. Read — read the passage slowly at least twice.
3. Observe — notice repeated words, commands, people, contrasts and cause/effect.
4. Context — examine the paragraph, chapter, book, audience and setting.
5. Interpret — determine what the text meant before applying it personally.
6. Compare — cross-reference Scripture and compare translations.
7. Meditate — slowly reflect on what is actually written.
8. Apply — identify one belief, action, correction or practice.
9. Pray — turn the truth into worship, confession and intercession.
10. Remember & teach — memorize the key truth and explain it in your own words.

Interpretation guardrails

Meaning before message.

  • Read the whole paragraph/chapter.
  • Identify speaker, audience and purpose.
  • Respect literary genre.
  • Compare translations.
  • Use cross-references carefully.
  • Separate clear teaching from inference.
  • Do not make an impression override the written text.

Study Academy — how this house reads the Word

Methods are servants. The aim is not a notebook. The aim is to see, to obey, to become like Christ, and to feed His people. Opening Sight walks the selected Scripture as living scenes; this Academy keeps that sight honest.

O — Observation

What does the text actually say? Mark repeating words, verbs, contrasts, lists, connecting words (therefore, but, so that). Name the people, places, and time. Do not interpret yet.

I — Interpretation

What did it mean to the first hearers? Genre, context, argument, and the book’s own purpose govern you. Scripture interprets Scripture. The obscure bows to the clear.

C — Context

Read the paragraph, the book, the covenant, and the whole canon. A verse cut from its soil can be made to say anything. Refuse isolated slogans.

T — Theology

What is here revealed of the Father, the Son, the Spirit, salvation, the church, the kingdom? Distill one true sentence you could preach without blushing.

A — Application

What must I believe, confess, start, stop, or suffer? Application is a decision with a weekday, not a mist of admiration.

P — Prayer

Pray the text back. Worship, confession, petition, intercession. Study that does not pray is anatomy without breath.

Genre map — do not read every book the same way

  • Narrative — plot, characters, God as the main actor. Meaning is in the whole scene, not a moral taped to one verse.
  • Law — covenant, holiness, how a redeemed people live near God. Ask how Christ fulfills and how the church now walks.
  • Poetry / Wisdom — image, parallelism, proverb as general truth not mechanical promise. Fear of the Lord is the gate.
  • Psalm — pray it. Notice address, complaint, trust, vow. The Psalter trains the affections.
  • Prophet — covenant lawsuit, warning, promise. The near event and the farther horizon. Christ is the Yes.
  • Gospel — Jesus in action. Follow the question: who is this Man? Read whole scenes. The cross and resurrection interpret everything.
  • Epistle — argument. Track therefore. Indicative before imperative (what God has done, then what we do).
  • Apocalypse — images that unveil, not a codebook for panic. Worship is the center. The Lamb wins.

Christ in all the Scriptures

Beginning at Moses and all the prophets, He expounded the things concerning Himself (Luke 24:27). Promise, pattern, presence: the Seed, the Lamb, the Priest, the King, the Servant, the Son. Never flatten the Old Testament into mere example, and never treat the New as if Christ arrived unannounced.

Prophetic guardrails

Sight is tested, not displayed. Does it agree with Scripture? Does it honor Christ crucified and risen? Does it produce His humility? The testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy. Impression that cannot be laid beside the Word must wait. Pedahzur Ministries: hold fast what is good.

Weekly formation — a simple master rhythm

  1. Lord’s Day — Worship with the church. One psalm prayed. Opening Sight on the preached text or Psalm 27:4.
  2. Monday — Gospel scene from the Life of Christ path. Observe the scene; do not skip to a slogan.
  3. Tuesday — Wisdom or Torah. Fear of the Lord. Copy one proverb by hand into the journal.
  4. Wednesday — Psalm + prayer. Lectio Divina on a short psalm.
  5. Thursday — Prophet. Warning and hope. Ask what God is saying to His people, not only to your feelings.
  6. Friday — Epistle. Track the argument. Fill O-I-C-T-A-P on the Deep Study Canvas. Memory Lab: one verse.
  7. Saturday — Review the week’s verse. Mark chapters read. Prepare Sunday. Rest without guilt.
