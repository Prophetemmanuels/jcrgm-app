Evangelism Conversation Questionnaire

A simple listening tool for outreach teams. Ask only what feels natural; this is a conversation aid, not an interview or a test.

Your choice comes first. Participation is optional; skip any question. We do not make prayer, help or welcome conditional on your answers. Only non-identifying totals may be used for outreach planning if you agree; written comments are not included in those totals. No contact details or person-level record should be kept without your clear permission. If you do not request follow-up, leave contact fields blank. Do not write sensitive personal information here. For anyone under 18, follow JCRGM safeguarding procedures and involve a parent/guardian as required.

Your faith journey and interests

1. Would you be comfortable talking about faith today?
2. Which best describes your experience with church or Christian faith?
3. What would you like to explore? (Choose any.)
4. Is there a question about faith you would like to discuss?Optional. Please leave private or sensitive details off this sheet.
5. Would you like someone to pray with you?
You may share a brief request aloud; there is no need to write sensitive details.
6. What next step, if any, would be helpful?
7. May JCRGM use only non-identifying totals from your selected answers to improve outreach?No names, contact details or written comments will be included. If you say no or are unsure, do not count or retain this sheet.

Optional follow-up — complete only if requested

8. Would you like JCRGM to contact you about a next step?
9. May JCRGM keep these contact details only to respond to the follow-up you requested?

If anonymous-summary permission is no or unclear, do not count or retain the answers. If either follow-up or record permission is no or unclear, do not keep or use contact details. Explain who handles consented records and follow JCRGM’s secure storage and deletion procedure. This local form sends nothing online.
