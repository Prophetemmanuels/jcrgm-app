CARD 1 — GREETER'S POCKET CARDDoor team - every service
THE 8-SECOND RULE

Smile - Eye contact - Welcome - Offer help, within 8 seconds of eye contact.

SAY
  • "You are welcome! Good morning."
  • "Is this your first time with us?"
  • "May I know your name?"
  • "Let me walk with you to the welcome desk."
  • "Please have a seat - I will get someone to sit with you."
IF IT IS A FIRST-TIMER

Greet -> escort -> introduce to the desk -> get the name -> find a host -> tell the Captain.

NEVER ON POST

Point instead of walking - eat or chew gum - ask about money, marriage or tribe - leave a door unmanned - embarrass a visitor.

AT THE END OF SERVICE

Reach every visitor within 60 seconds. Thank them by name. Invite them back next Sunday.

CARD 2 — 48-HOUR FOLLOW-UP CALL CARDFollow-up team - call every visitor
OPENING

"Good morning, may I speak to [Name]? ... Hello [Name], this is [Your name] from JCRGM. We worshipped together on Sunday - I am calling to thank you personally for coming, and to see how you are."

ASK

Did you travel safely? - How did you find the service? - Was anything a blessing to you? - May we pray about anything with you?

INVITE

Join us Sunday: Morning Worship 09:00-12:30 and Counselling & Prophetic Service 14:00-18:30. If you need transport, tell me and we will arrange.

NEVER

Preach at them - ask for money - promise anything - discuss other members - call after 19:00.

LOG WITHIN 10 MINUTES

Date - reached / not reached (why) - duration - needs shared - next action - next contact date - your initials.

3 ATTEMPTS RULE

Call, then WhatsApp the same day; again next day; again on day 4. After 3 attempts mark 'unreachable - member to assist', never 'failed'.

CARD 4 — USHER'S SEATING CARDUshering team - order, comfort, watchfulness
SEAT FIRST

The elderly - mothers with babies - visitors - people with a disability - then fill front-to-back in neat blocks.

NEVER FOR FIRST-TIMERS

The back row, or behind a pillar. Reserve front-middle seats for visitors who came with children (quick exit).

DURING THE SERVICE

Stand at the back, silent, watching. No walking up and down, no chatting, no phone light.

ALERT FOR

Anyone weeping, unwell or fainting - wandering children - an empty visitor seat - any disturbance - anyone slipping out early (empty seat rule).

AT THE END

Open the aisle for the altar call, guide responders forward, escort visitors to the welcome desk, and apply the 60-second rule.

CARD 7 — WELCOME DESK CARDReception & registration team
SET UP BEFORE DOORS OPEN

Table + cloth - visitor cards + 6 pens - register book - QR stand - welcome packs - water - tissue - first-aid box - children's tags - referral slips.

STAFF AT PEAK

1 greeter (front) - 1 writer (cards) - 1 gifter (packs) - 1 floater to escort visitors and fetch what is needed.

ASK FOR

Name - phone / WhatsApp - area - first time here? - who invited you - children - prayer request - consent to contact.

THE TWO-MINUTE RULE

Never keep a visitor standing for more than two minutes. If the queue is long, seat them and take the card to them.

PROTECT THE DATA

All cards go to the Data Officer the same day. Never read a number aloud, never share details, and mark DO NOT CONTACT when asked.

NEVER

Force a card - ask about money, marriage or tribe - promise money, jobs or prophecy - leave the desk unattended.

CARD 8 — HOME VISIT CARDVisitation team - 7-day follow-up
BEFORE YOU GO

Confirm by phone - send TWO people (same gender where possible) - tell the Coordinator who, where and when - carry a small gift, a Bible and a contact card - pray together as a team.

DURING THE VISIT (30-40 MINUTES)

Greet the whole household - one Scripture, one testimony, one prayer - answer their questions - invite them to the cell and to Sunday - note real needs.

NEVER

Interrogate - inspect their lifestyle - borrow anything - counsel an abuse or crisis case alone - arrive unannounced - stay too long.

AFTER THE VISIT

Write and hand in the visit report the same day - log any referral - arrange the cell introduction - confirm their second Sunday.
