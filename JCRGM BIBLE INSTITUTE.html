Study Tools

A practical workflow for serious Bible study: observation → interpretation → application → prayer.

OIA Study Method

  1. Observe: Who, what, where, when, why? Note repetition, contrast and context.
  2. Interpret: What did the author mean in context? Compare Scripture with Scripture.
  3. Apply: What must I believe, change, obey, practice or teach?

Context Checklist

Read the paragraph, chapter and book. Identify author, audience, occasion, genre, historical setting and the flow of argument.

Cross-Reference Strategy

Compare parallel passages, quotations, allusions and repeated biblical themes. Let clearer passages help with less-clear passages.

Word Study

Check the word's meaning in its immediate context, then consult original-language tools and multiple translations. Never build doctrine on a dictionary definition alone.
