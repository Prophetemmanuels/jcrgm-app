JCRGM Church System

Vision • Policies • Departments • Kingdom Culture

Vision

To raise a generation of kingdom ambassadors who are spiritually mature, rooted in the Word, and empowered by the Holy Spirit to demonstrate the reality of Christ in every dimension of life. To establish a people of prayer, holiness, and revelation who walk in dominion, manifest the supernatural, and bring transformation to families, communities, and nations. To equip and release leaders with excellence, integrity, and divine authority to advance the Kingdom of God globally, impacting this generation and generations to come.

Mission

  • Preach the Gospel with power
  • Disciple believers
  • Raise leaders
  • Demonstrate God's power

Core Values

  • Holiness
  • Prayer
  • Unity
  • Excellence
  • Faith

General Church Rules

  • Attend services consistently
  • Respect leadership
  • Avoid gossip and division
  • Live a holy life

Financial Policy

  • Tithes must be given faithfully
  • Offerings are required in services
  • Support church projects

Department System

All departments must align with the church vision and contribute to spiritual growth.
  • Choir – Lead worship and spiritual atmosphere
  • Media – Handle sound, visuals, and recordings
  • Ushers – Maintain order and welcome people
  • Evangelism – Win souls
  • Protocol – Handle leadership coordination

Leadership Rules

  • Leaders must be spiritually mature
  • Lead by example
  • Train others
  • Remain accountable

Discipline & Restoration

  • Correction is done in love
  • Persistent misconduct leads to discipline
  • Restoration is always the goal

Member Commitment

Every member must uphold the vision, follow leadership, and actively build God's Kingdom.
