Vision • Policies • Departments • Kingdom Culture

Vision

To raise a generation of kingdom ambassadors who are spiritually mature, rooted in the Word, and empowered by the Holy Spirit to demonstrate the reality of Christ in every dimension of life.

To establish a people of prayer, holiness, and revelation who walk in dominion, manifest the supernatural, and bring transformation to families, communities, and nations.

To equip and release leaders with excellence, integrity, and divine authority to advance the Kingdom of God globally, impacting this generation and generations to come.

Mission

Mission (How We Fulfill the Vision)

1. To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ with clarity, power, and demonstration of the Holy Spirit, bringing salvation and transformation to lives.

2. To disciple believers through sound teaching of the Word, mentorship, and spiritual training, raising them into maturity and stability in Christ.

3. To establish a strong culture of prayer, fasting, and intimacy with God, producing believers who are spiritually sensitive and empowered.

4. To identify, train, and raise leaders in every department who will serve with excellence, integrity, and accountability.

5. To build and strengthen families through biblical principles, counseling, and godly relationship teachings.

6. To promote unity, love, and service within the church, creating a strong and healthy spiritual community.

7. To engage in consistent evangelism and outreach programs, winning souls and expanding the Kingdom of God.

8. To demonstrate the power of God through healing, deliverance, and manifestations of the Holy Spirit.

9. To equip members with life skills, wisdom, and discipline that enable them to succeed in both spiritual and practical life.

10. To use media, technology, and innovation to spread the Gospel and impact a wider audience globally.

Core Values

1. Holiness and Righteous Living: We commit to live pure, holy lives that honor God in every thought, word, and action.

2. Prayer and Dependence on God: We maintain a lifestyle of prayer, fasting, and reliance on the Holy Spirit for guidance, power, and breakthroughs.

3. Love, Unity, and Service: We prioritize love and unity within the body of Christ, serving one another and our community with humility and compassion.

4. Excellence in Ministry: We strive for excellence in all areas—worship, teaching, administration, and outreach—bringing glory to God through quality and diligence.

5. Faith and Obedience: We walk by faith, obeying God’s Word even when it challenges us, trusting His promises to fulfill His plans through us.

6. Integrity and Accountability: We act with honesty, transparency, and responsibility, holding ourselves and each other accountable to Godly standards.

7. Generosity and Kingdom Giving: We are faithful in tithes, offerings, and supporting Kingdom projects, understanding that giving brings blessing and multiplies God’s work.

8. Discipleship and Mentorship: We invest in raising others spiritually through teaching, mentoring, and developing future leaders.

9. Evangelism and Outreach: We are committed to reaching the lost, spreading the Gospel locally and globally, and demonstrating God’s love through action.

10. Spiritual Growth and Continuous Learning: We commit to lifelong learning of God’s Word, growing in wisdom, knowledge, and spiritual maturity.

Financial Policy

1. Faithful Tithing: Members are expected to give their tithe consistently, recognizing that it belongs to the Lord and supports the work of His Kingdom.

2. Freewill Offerings: Members are encouraged to give offerings beyond their tithe, as led by God’s Spirit, to support church projects, missions, and charitable work.

3. Support for Church Projects: All members are called to participate in the financing of church expansion, community outreach, and other Kingdom initiatives.

4. Accountability and Transparency: All financial contributions are recorded and managed responsibly. Church leaders maintain full transparency to ensure trust and integrity in handling resources.

5. Stewardship and Generosity: Members are encouraged to practice wise stewardship in their personal finances and to live generous lives that honor God and bless others.

6. Special Kingdom Contributions: Periodically, members may be invited to support special initiatives, events, or mission work. Participation is voluntary but highly encouraged.

7. Financial Counseling and Guidance: The church provides support and guidance to help members manage their resources in a Godly manner, promoting financial wisdom and discipline.

8. Giving in Faith: All contributions are made as an act of faith, trusting God to multiply resources and bless the giver spiritually, materially, and socially.
