HOW TO PUT IT ON YOUR WEBSITE
-----------------------------
1. Upload these three files to the same folder as your other pages
   (membership.html, jcrgm-support-team.html, etc.):
       index.html
       visitors-welcome.html
       welcome-cards-print.html
2. Also upload the PDF files (or keep them in a sub-folder named "cards" and update the links).
3. Open index.html — the new "Visitors & Welcome Ministry" group is near the top of the hub.
   Visitors & Welcome Ministry tile opens the full manual.

NOTES
-----
* The PDFs are also EMBEDDED inside visitors-welcome.html, so the Download buttons work
  even offline, on a phone, or when a page is opened from a flash drive.
* Printing: everything prints cleanly in black and white. For the manual, open
  visitors-welcome.html and press "Print / Save PDF" (all training modules open automatically).
* Before printing the cards, fill in the blank fields you want to keep:
  emergency numbers (manual Section 12.2), coordinator and captain phone numbers,
  and any names.
* Suggested first print run: 40 copies of Card 1 (greeters/ushers), 20 of Card 2 (callers),
  100 copies of Card 3 (visitors' pathway, for the gift packs), 4 each of Cards 5–8
  (desk drawer and team leaders).
