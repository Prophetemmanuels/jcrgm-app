A strong teaching culture

Let the same truth travel through church and groups

Hear · understand · practise · review

A theme becomes formative when people hear Scripture, understand it in context, discuss it honestly, practise it in daily life and revisit the fruit. The Teaching tab includes a complete lesson method and 12-session series; each course now includes expanded teaching guides, discussion, weekly practice and lesson-specific YouTube searches.

1 · GatherPreach or teach the biblical text clearly in a church gathering.
2 · DiscussUse the same theme for Bible class or small-group questions.
3 · PractiseChoose one safe, specific action for the week.
4 · ReviewStart next session with reflection, care and the next faithful step.
Facilitator standard: every lesson should include a passage read in context, one clear teaching aim, an outline, open discussion questions, an application, a prayer option and any safeguarding/referral note. Never shame people for silence, questions, doubt or different learning needs.

Suggested JCRGM 90-day rollout

Leadership-led · congregation-centred

Days 1–30 · Listen

Review doctrine, constitution, current teams, safeguarding, privacy and finances. Listen to workers, members and newcomers. Choose three priorities with accountable owners.

Days 31–60 · Teach & equip

Start the church-wide teaching series; choose a new-believer pathway; train facilitators, outreach workers and safeguarding leads.

Days 61–90 · Serve & review

Run a listening-led outreach; review group participation, care, finance controls and worker wellbeing; publish an appropriate next-quarter plan.
