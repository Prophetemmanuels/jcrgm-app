🔥 Today's Prayer Assignment

Preparing today's five prayer targets for each order…

Step 1

Still yourself

Sit, breathe, and wait two minutes. Do not begin with a list — begin with the Lord.

Step 2

Read the Word

Read each Scripture aloud before you pray it, so the prayer is shaped by the text.

Step 3

Pray the target

Move through the prayer points, then pray the guided prayer in your own words.

Step 4

Seal it

Speak the declaration aloud, tick the target as completed, and give God the thanks.

    📖 Daily Prophetic Declarations

    Spoken after the twenty-five targets are complete, or at any point in the day. Declare them standing, aloud, and in faith — the Word of God never returns void.

    • 🔥I am led by the Spirit of God today.
    • 🔥My spiritual ears are open to hear the voice of the Lord.
    • 🔥The presence of God goes with me and gives me rest.
    • 🔥I walk in holiness, wisdom and obedience.
    • 🔥I will fulfil the purpose of God for my life.
    • 🔥No weapon formed against me shall prosper.
    • 🔥The Holy Spirit orders my steps and my words.
    • 🔥The Word of God is alive and powerful in me.
    • 🔥My household is covered, and we serve the Lord.
    • 🔥JCRGM shall stand in truth, power and glory.
